## Execution environment

- **Who runs what.** The orchestrator runs this plan inline; it is not dispatched to a `gsd-executor` as a whole, because an executor has no Agent tool and its own reading does not count as independent. The orchestrator spawns `gsd-code-reviewer` with the Agent tool, one fresh agent per round, with the brief as its prompt. Fixes are applied by a `gsd-executor` the orchestrator dispatches with a fix-only prompt (the finding IDs with their verbatim `### ID: title` lines, the files it may touch, the owning checks to re-run, and the rule that it does not review). GSD lives under `~/.claude`: any snippet that requires `./gsd-core/bin/lib/...` is pointed at `~/.claude/gsd-core/bin/lib/`.
- Plan 32-01's Execution environment applies (fixed SHAs, shell traps, evidence format). `P32_BASE = 8dcbbfc1ced091b49abc6d444b562d591ffc28f2` is the review's `diff_base`.
- **Files under review (three).** `docs/ROLLOUT-v1.7.0_gg5.md`, `CHANGELOG.md` (the `1.7.0_gg5` section), `docs/FORK-DELTA.md` (the diff since P32_BASE and its consistency with the rest of the file). Evidence map: `32-COMMAND-EVIDENCE.md` and the Phase 30 and 31 evidence it cites.
- **Fix-text rule.** Every command, number, run id or wording a reviewer proposes is checked against the files it describes and the evidence before it is applied (Phase 24: a proposed key form was wrong). A changed command is re-run and recorded as a new evidence entry from `### E-30:`, `Matches text: yes`; a changed rollback step re-runs `/tmp/gsd32/rollback-demo.sh` on a dry-run merge (plan 32-02 Task 3's verify command 1).
- **Owning checks, re-run verbatim after any fix:** NOTE-FRAME-OK, ITEMS-2-4-OK, ROLLBACK-REHEARSED-OK and NOTE-FINAL-OK (32-02) for the note; GG5-IDENTITY-OK, FORK-DELTA-CI-GROUP-OK, CHANGELOG-GG5-OK and RAW-NUMBERS-GG5-OK (32-03) for the CHANGELOG and FORK-DELTA.
- **Disposition values (PT-11)** are `fixed`, `rejected` and `deferred`; `open` is not allowed at the end. `rejected` carries its reason in the Source cell (no `|` in it). `deferred` is only for a finding whose fix needs a protected path (a workflow, a test, the build), and its Source cell names the reason, the owner and the re-open trigger; the finding is also listed as `### 32-REVIEW <ID>: <title>` with those three fields under `## Carried to the milestone close` in the disposition file, for `/gsd-complete-milestone`.
