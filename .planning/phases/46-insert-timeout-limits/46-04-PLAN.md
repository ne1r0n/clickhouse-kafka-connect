On client V1 the three insert methods wait with `future.get(<bound>, MILLISECONDS)` and turn a `TimeoutException` into a `RetriableException` (``ClickHouse insert timed out after <n> ms (QueryId: [<id>])``) after cancelling the future, and the existing try-with-resources releases the connection. Both are skipped, through `ClickHouseHelperClient.insertBoundMs`, whenever `jdbcConnectionProperties` names `socket_timeout` (any value, `0` included). Through `ProxySinkTask.put` both are retriable under `errors.tolerance=none` and `all` with no DLQ record.
