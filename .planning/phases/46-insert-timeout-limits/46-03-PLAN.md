Rollout consequence this plan creates (stated in plans 46-04 and 46-05): on client V1 the bound is a cap on the whole wait for the insert response, so any V1 insert still running when `clickhouseClientInsertTimeoutMs` elapses (quiet, progress-streaming or slow) now fails and is retried; before, a quiet V1 insert already failed at the library's 30 s inactivity limit, but a progress-streaming or slowly sent one ran to its end. The server may still commit a timed-out insert. V1 cannot separate the write phase from the response phase, which is why U-2 bounds the whole wait.
