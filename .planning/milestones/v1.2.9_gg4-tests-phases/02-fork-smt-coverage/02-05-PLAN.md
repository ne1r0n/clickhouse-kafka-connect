<objective>
Prove, against a real ClickHouse `latest` container with client V2, that each of the four fork SMTs
puts records where they belong: filtered Debezium operations never arrive, headers land in a map
column, a header-derived field lands in its column, and JSON-string output lands across typed
columns plus a `JSON` column.

Purpose: the unit tests in plans 02-01..02-04 prove what each transform returns. Only a sink-level
test proves the connector then writes that shape into ClickHouse without the writer silently
dropping, mangling, or rejecting it. TOF-06 in particular is the only place the drop-on-null
contract — the mechanism that deletes production CDC rows — is exercised through the real insert
path.

Output: one new ClickHouse-backed test class,
`src/test/java/com/clickhouse/kafka/connect/sink/SmtSinkLandingTest.java`, covering TOF-06,
HDRS-09, HDR-07 and CONV-08.
