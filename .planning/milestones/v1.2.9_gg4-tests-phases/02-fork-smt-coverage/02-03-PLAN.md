<objective>
Pin every unit-level behaviour of `HeadersToValue` (HDRS-01..HDRS-08), close the stale
`valueSchema` cache, and correct the HDRS-04 requirement wording that currently misstates the
`configure()` failure contract.

headers into a ClickHouse map column. Its empty-map, null-entry, duplicate-key and collision
behaviours are all currently untested, and its schema cache silently drops fields when an upstream
Debezium schema evolves.
